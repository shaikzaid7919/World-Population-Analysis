# World-Population-Analysis

## Objective:
Explore world population trends from 1970 to 2022, identify growth patterns, and understand world population distribution across continents and countries.

## Dataset:
World Population Dataset containing population, density, area, growth rate, etc.

## Tools:
- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly)

## Key Insights:
- Asia holds 60% of world population.
- Africa has the highest growth rate from 1970 to 2022.
- China and India together account for 35% of the world population.
